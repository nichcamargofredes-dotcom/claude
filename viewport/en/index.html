Process

From the first call to a live site.

You follow every step and talk directly to the people building your site, from quote to launch.

  1. Step 1ConversationWe learn about your business, your audience and what you want people to feel when they open your site.
  2. Step 2Proposal and layoutWe send a fixed quote and the first visual draft for you to approve or adjust.
  3. Step 3DevelopmentWe build the site with its animations and test it on desktop, tablet and phone.
  4. Step 4Launch and supportWe publish it on your domain, connect WhatsApp and Google, and stay around for adjustments.
